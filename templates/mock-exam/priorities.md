## Conversion into practice

For each daily-eligible priority:

1. record or update the error in `data/error_log.csv`;
2. link an active structure when useful;
3. create or reactivate a recent-error challenge;
4. ask for independent production in a different context;
5. move it to spaced review only after the configured study-day and success requirements.

Long synthesis or paragraph-level goals remain full-production objectives rather than short daily
cards.
